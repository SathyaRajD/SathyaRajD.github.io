Operator profile · About

Beyond Playing. Into Quality.

Professional Game QA starts with knowing exactly what a game is supposed to do. From there the work is investigation: finding where behaviour departs from expectation, reproducing it until the cause is narrow enough to describe, judging who it affects, and handing engineers something they can act on.

I have done that across a live match-3 title over four years, WebGL builds on Facebook Gaming, Unity SDK upgrades across iOS, Android and Web, and most recently pre-release GPU hardware at Intel, where the question was whether upscaling and frame generation held up in real AAA games before players saw them.
